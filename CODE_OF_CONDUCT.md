# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing viewpoints,
constructive feedback, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on community
well-being. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention,
trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, harassment, and publishing
another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement and scope

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that
violate these standards. This policy applies in project spaces and when a
person officially represents the project.

Request a private contact channel from the repository owner through their
public GitHub profile to report an incident. Do not include sensitive incident
details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed fairly and with respect for
the reporter's privacy.

Consequences may progress from correction, to warning, temporary ban, or
permanent ban according to severity and recurrence.
