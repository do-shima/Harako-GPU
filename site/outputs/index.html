Analysis outputs

Outputs are recorded with their role and verification status

The artifact manifest and run status show whether an expected output was generated and verified. A filename alone is not treated as evidence of a successful run.

Preprocessing and alignment

Processed FASTQ

Paired *.fastp.fastq.gz files are accompanied by fastp JSON and HTML reports. Salmon uses these processed FASTQ files only; it is not run from a transcriptome BAM.

BAM / BAI

The coordinate-sorted BAM and its index include quickcheck, header and reference checks, record checks, and the result of deep verification.

STAR outputs

Log.final.out, SJ.out.tab, ReadsPerGene.out.tab, and the alignment transcriptome BAM are recorded together with their provenance.

Counts, abundance, and matrices

FeatureCounts

GENCODE 49 biotype QC uses the fixed exon/gene_type settings. This output complements rather than replaces STAR GeneCounts.

Salmon

For every selected profile, Harako-GPU records quant.sf, quant.genes.sf, timing, version, container image, index, options, library type, and related metadata.

Matrices and concordance

Outputs include transcript- and gene-level TPM, NumReads, and effective-length matrices. Compare-both also reports numerical differences and rank concordance between the two Salmon versions.

Policy for storing completed runs

  • Active output and work remain on a local ext4 SSD.
  • After a successful run, users may explicitly create and verify a PAX terminal-results archive on WD Gold with harako-gpu-terminal-results-archive-v1.
  • Work from a failed or resumable run remains on the SSD until the issue is resolved or the run is explicitly abandoned.
  • The complete Nextflow work directory may be removed only through a separate user action after the archive has been verified.

Harako-GPU v0.1.0-alpha.1 does not archive or delete data automatically, and WD Gold is never used as the Nextflow workDir.

The archive receipt verifies the members and checksums of the archive. It does not authorize scientific interpretation or publication of the data.
