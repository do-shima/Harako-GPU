Terminal-results archive policy

  • Active output/work: local ext4 SSD.
  • Successful terminal run: optionally create and verify a PAX terminal-results archive on WD Gold using harako-gpu-terminal-results-archive-v1.
  • Failed or resumable run: retain work on SSD until resolved or explicitly abandoned.
  • Full Nextflow work may be removed only by a separate explicit operator action after archive verification.

Harako-GPU v0.1.0-alpha.1 does not perform background archive or automatic deletion and never uses WD Gold as Nextflow workDir.

The archive receipt verifies members and identity; it does not authorize scientific interpretation or data publication.
